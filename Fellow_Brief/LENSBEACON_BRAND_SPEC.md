# LensBeacon Apple Fellow Brand Specification

## Identity
Name: LensBeacon
Tagline: Spot camera glasses. On your terms.
Personality: calm, precise, private, factual, technically confident.

## Icon concept
A lens/beacon mark. Two opposing arcs surround a single optical center.
Do NOT add glasses, shields, warning triangles, checkmarks, magnifying glasses,
Bluetooth logos, people, cameras, or surveillance imagery.

## Color tokens

Teal: #10B981
Cool Gray: #E5EAF2
Background: #F8FAFC
Primary Text: #0B1F33
Secondary Text: #64748B

## Icon rules
- Full-bleed background.
- No artificial drop shadow.
- No text in the icon.
- Preserve the central optical silhouette.
- Must remain recognizable at 29–40 pt.
- Keep the mark visually simple; do not add detail to solve small-size loss.
- iOS/iPadOS master: 1024 x 1024.
- watchOS master: 1088 x 1088.
- Use Icon Composer for layered platform-native icon output.
- Do not independently redraw the iPhone/iPad/Watch marks.

## Product tone
Never use alarmist/security-panic language.
Prefer "detected", "possible", "likely", "strong", "evidence", "nearby".
Avoid claims of certainty or directional precision.
